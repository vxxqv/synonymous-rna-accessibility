# Joint-function pilot: verified decision

The frozen validation milestone was not met. Six reserved squares in six groups had complete measurements, below the required ten squares in eight groups. The additive predictor also failed the continuous accuracy benchmark. A narrower measured-data observation survives: one reserved square has individually accessible paths for protein output and growth, but no path preserving both, consistently across all twelve deposited-series scenarios and the declared empirical-range test. This is a finite, descriptive observation within one processed reporter library. It does not establish a folding mechanism, calibrated biological robustness, an evolutionary barrier or improved function.

Reserved predictions were separately sealed and committed before reserved targets. The protocol hash is `0f51ccc808559ea69df3bdf294520d81babb67bf14ca92ce2abb6fcc665760ac`. No predictor, threshold, exclusion, group weight, coverage minimum or success rule changed after outcomes.

Each eligible square requires four deposited protein series and three growth series at all four corners. Trait thresholds are the lower endpoint means, with weak acceptance. Both endpoint diagonals are retained. Twelve whole-series combinations are sensitivity scenarios, not twelve independent biological replicates. Empirical min/max ranges define deterministic sets with uncalibrated biological coverage; they are not confidence intervals. Their certificate uses fixed low-end endpoint thresholds, not thresholds recomputed under every possible perturbation.

The reserved additive error exceeds adjacent mean by 8.84% for protein and 13.42% for growth. Its gains over three-corner mean are 32.79% and 8.58%, so three of four required 10% comparisons fail. Removing influential group 277 makes all four continuous comparisons pass on five cases, still below coverage. Selecting that deletion would be an invalid rescue; the full-arm failure remains decisive. Every deletion fails the complete validation screen.

Additive completion correctly classifies the eleven scored development cases and six scored reserved cases; the reserved set contains two positives and four negatives. Both averaging comparators predict no positives. An adversarial review found a structural reason after evaluation, which sharply limits the classification comparison.

Let opposite endpoints be s and t, the observed intermediate z, and predicted intermediate c. For each trait j, the requirement is tau_j = min(s_j, t_j). If c_j is any convex combination of s_j, t_j and z_j, then z_j >= tau_j implies c_j >= tau_j. The acceptable traits of z are therefore a subset of those of c. If each trait has a path through one of these two intermediates, c must pass every trait and provides a joint path. Consequently neither mean baseline can predict a joint-only restriction in exact arithmetic. Three-corner mean need not pass every trait; when it fails, the same trait fails at z. Additive completion uses a negative coefficient and is not constrained by this argument.

For example, with s = t = (0,0) and z = (1,-1), additive completion gives c = (-1,1), producing opposing accessible paths. This is an exactly additive antagonistic field, not epistasis. Three-corner completion gives (1/3,-1/3), which disconnects the second trait. The claim follows from elementary convexity and is presented as a benchmark limitation, not an asserted new general theorem. Floating arithmetic at exact ties can violate the exact nesting relation; current labels were independently checked and no observed change was required. No replacement comparator was fitted on these exposed outcomes.

## Independent verification and research decision

The frozen independent core verifier agreed with 18,458 exact and 2,160 arithmetic comparisons in development and 12,049 exact and 1,761 arithmetic comparisons in the reserved arm. It independently reconstructed all 336 exact threshold cells and 432 series scenarios across the two arms. Labels, coverage, thresholds and tolerance values matched exactly; arithmetic comparison tolerances did not change scientific thresholds. Separate supplement verification reproduced every group deletion, 127 extrema summaries per arm and 28 component/scenario/range contexts per arm, with maximum discrepancy 1.78e-15. These checks corroborate the calculations, not the original experimental processing or biological interpretation.

The overall decision is information-limited validation failure, with descriptive examples retained. It is not a biological null. The independent-study, calibrated uncertainty and practical-impact requirements remain unsatisfied. The next research cycle must use a genuinely new validation resource or resolve a missing measurement contract; it cannot tune against either exposed arm. Priority goes to exact matched-context identity and an identifiable question beyond already established GC and intron effects. A current-method and closest-prior review precedes any new outcome model or expensive analysis.
