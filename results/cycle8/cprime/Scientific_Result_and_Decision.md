# cPRIME: complete marker-context result

The deposited RNA-to-DNA proxy retains marker sensitivity in 19 of 60 intended-label/cell comparisons under the declared preparation envelopes. Seven comparisons have a minimum dispersion of at least twofold. This is a complete descriptive audit of a fixed finite cohort, not a population estimate or an isolated-edit effect. The originating study already demonstrated marker non-neutrality; the additional contribution is a complete, exact, uncertainty-explicit audit and its limits.

All 396 paired measurements are usable, representing 132 state/cell envelopes, 66 canonical local motif states and 30 intended labels in two cells. There are no missing states or source-score inconsistencies. The maximum absolute discrepancy between the deposited rounded score and the independently recomputed paired ratio is 8.14e-13, below the frozen file-consistency tolerance.

Every row uses the same 60 dependent comparisons and five exon tests. Widening replaces each envelope [L,U] by [L/gamma,U*gamma]. It is a declared assumption, not an estimated error rate. All continuous bounds and the fixed fold grid are available in the complete tables.

The primary exon-17 certificate reverses two markers of one intended label between cells. The stored exon-27 certificate instead spans three intended labels. Neither common-order exclusion survives the first declared widening factor, 1.25. Feasibility of a common unrestricted order does not establish a codon-additive model. No accessibility graph or barrier is identified by this marker comparison.

The independent reconstruction agrees exactly with all 396 source rows, 132 envelopes, 240 group/widening records and 20 exon/widening tests. It checks 872 edge-provenance entries and 456 feasible-response values. Its original count-versus-list summary failure and the disclosed schema-only adapter are retained. This verifies computation, not biological calibration or independent experimental replication.

A post-primary precision check preserves every frozen decision. None of the 19 strict lower-bound positives, seven twofold lower-bound positives or five stored cycle inequalities is within relative 1e-12 of its decision boundary. One upper bound is 2+1/468840859893519. Its frozen upper-at-most-two flag remains false, but the approximately 2.13e-15 excess has no asserted biological meaning. This diagnostic reporting scale is not a new uncertainty model.

The strongest competing explanations are context-specific marker main effects and differences in preparation normalization, local-motif recovery, transcript visibility or unphased backgrounds. A single common positive exon/cell normalization constant cancels from these ratios and ranks; preparation- or state-dependent factors do not. Neither the dispersion nor the order reversal requires an edit-marker interaction.

The single most useful missing source record is an authoritative mapping from these 396 processed rows to paired RNA/DNA library accessions, preparation identities and normalization totals. It would make shared offsets and dependence auditable. It would not alone identify total RNA, an isolated intended-edit effect, full haplotypes or processed proteins.
