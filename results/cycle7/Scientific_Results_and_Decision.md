## Why the assay does not yet identify inclusion biology

An intensity model factors the normalized proxy into relative included fraction multiplied by relative effective template delivery, total RNA and output/input recovery. Common scales and source-template abundance cancel. Genotype-specific factors do not. A steady-state included fraction is not a kinetic splicing rate, and a ratio of noisy counts is not automatically an unbiased ratio of intensities.

With unrestricted positive genotype multipliers, any positive single proxy field can be explained with constant true inclusion. Under a bound `1/kappa <= b_v <= kappa`, `b_reference = 1`, existence of one additive-monotone inclusion field and one multiplier mapping it into the original empirical box is equivalent to feasibility in the anchored multiplicatively expanded box. This equivalence handles zeros and unbounded intervals explicitly.

Accordingly, the computed gamma is the smallest formal nuisance allowance for **one model-compatible completion of the box**. It is not one persistent correction fitting all 27 observed fields, a measured error rate, or a calibration obtained from technical input replicates. An independently verified full-square counterexample demonstrates that box compatibility need not imply simultaneous correction of all scenarios. True-inclusion model exclusion would require an independently supported nuisance bound and justified coverage of the true field, neither of which is supplied by these empirical ranges.
