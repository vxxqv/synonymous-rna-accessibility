# Source terms and attribution

The MIT license applies to the new computational source and documentation in this repository. It does not relicense third-party source records.

The original PAIR-MaP subset is CC0, as stated in the versioned record [10.6084/m9.figshare.9978596.v1](https://doi.org/10.6084/m9.figshare.9978596.v1). Source bytes and hashes are preserved under `data/pairmap/`. The GenBank reference is U00096.2. Study attribution: Mustoe et al. (2019), [10.1073/pnas.1905491116](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.1905491116).

The primary transcript models derive from Mustoe et al. (2018), [10.1016/j.cell.2018.02.034](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cell.2018.02.034), through a commit-pinned BackofenLab/ShaKer mirror. No explicit mirror license was found. The complete mirror and publisher archive are not bundled; source retrieval URLs/checksums, factual coordinate mappings, selected model inputs and derived results are provided for reproducibility. No CC0 claim is made for those third-party records, and their original terms remain applicable. The original CT partner audit remains incomplete.

The Goodman and Kudla reporter workbooks are not redistributed. Their source and exclusion audit is recorded, and no reporter prediction result is claimed.

ViennaRNA, NetworkX, SymPy, Matplotlib, NumPy, Biopython, pytest, R and the compiler retain their own licenses and are installed separately. No proprietary fonts or third-party article PDFs are included.

The CNOT3 gene-level workbook is Supplementary Table S2 of Carneiro and Palhano, [10.1002/2211-5463.70277](https://doi.org/10.1002/2211-5463.70277), distributed with the article under [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). It reuses the experiment reported by Zhu et al., [10.1126/science.adq8587](https://doi.org/10.1126/science.adq8587), with original sources [GSE268322](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE268322) and [GSE268324](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE268324). The reanalysis is not independent experimental replication. The original SLAM workbook is represented here by its identifier-only inventory and source hash; unneeded trajectory values are not redistributed in this branch.

The pinned reference transcript FASTA and derived coding/UTR features use [GENCODE human release 48](https://www.gencodegenes.org/human/release_48.html). GENCODE describes its project data as [open access](https://www.gencodegenes.org/pages/data_access.html). These third-party reference records retain their source terms and attribution; the repository MIT license does not relicense them. Exact release files, checksums, transcript conventions and the distinction from expressed isoforms are recorded in the CNOT3 computational protocol and provenance.
