# Correlated-prior qualification for the preserved proof

This is a prose clarification to `prior_and_proof.md`, not a change to the frozen experiment, its source or any numerical result. It makes precise the existing review's zero-probability qualification.

Let Omega contain every logically possible Boolean world allowed by the observation contract, and let Gamma(s) contain all worlds compatible with an observed state s. The prior mu may assign zero probability to some worlds. A universal stopping certificate still requires the connectivity answer to be constant on all of Gamma(s), including its zero-prior worlds. Constancy only on the positive-prior support is insufficient. For example, a prior concentrated on the all-absent world does not certify separation before observing the unknown vertices: other logically compatible worlds may connect the endpoints.

Posterior probabilities and conditional expected additional costs are defined by ordinary conditioning only at histories with mu(Gamma(s)) > 0. For those histories, an action's positive-mass outcome branches are weighted by their posterior masses. Zero-mass branches contribute nothing to that conditional expectation and can be omitted from its sum. This omission is a probability calculation, not permission to stop without a universal certificate.

Under the preserved action contract, every singleton remains available and every chosen action reveals at least one new coordinate at a fixed positive price. On zero-mass branches one can specify a counterfactual continuation that observes the remaining unknown coordinates until a universal certificate is reached. Such a continuation does not change the prior expected cost, but preserves correctness in every logically possible world. No posterior or uniquely determined conditional expected value is claimed at a zero-mass history. Induction for the expected-cost optimum applies on positive-mass histories, with zero-mass branches treated as above.

The actual 384-case experiment uses independent Bernoulli probabilities 1/4, 1/2 and 3/4, so all 64 worlds and every compatible partial state have positive probability. Its original recurrence, all saved state values, every world trajectory, the proof's full-support interpretation and all exact comparisons remain unchanged. Correlated priors and zero-support priors were not computationally tested. This clarification supplies no noisy-observation, Bayesian-certificate, calibration, assay-cost or general scalability claim.
