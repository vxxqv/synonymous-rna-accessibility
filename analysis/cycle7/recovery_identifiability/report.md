The detailed claim cards, assumptions, proofs and counterexamples are in `claim_and_proof.md`. The word “bias” refers to a nuisance relative to the inclusion estimand: genotype-dependent total RNA can be genuine biology rather than a technical defect. A bound only on instrument/RT efficiency does not bound the entire nuisance product if total RNA remains unrestricted. Also, steady-state exon inclusion is not a kinetic splice-choice rate when isoform lifetimes differ.

## Observation chain

At the intensity level, write input as `alpha_t*D_v*qI_vt`, output as `beta_r*D_v*e_vr*a_vr*p_vr*qO_vr`. Here D is source-template representation, e effective delivery, a total relevant RNA/template, p included fraction and q recovery. Then

`R_vrt = (p_vr/p_wr) * [(e_vr*a_vr*qO_vr)/(e_wr*a_wr*qO_wr)] * [qI_wt/qI_vt]`.

Shared scales cancel; every displayed genotype ratio remains. A count ratio is a random estimator, not generally an unbiased ratio of mean intensities. The existing empirical range supplies no stochastic coverage model. Constant true inclusion plus arbitrary positive recovery can reproduce any positive single ordering even if primitive recoveries are constrained to at most1: a common rescaling makes all recovery probabilities legal while their relative ratios remain unchanged.

For multiple preparations, an explicitly persistent factor is one b_v used everywhere. Genotype-by-preparation factors b_vr are a different model. Two fields(1,2) and(1,1/2) can each be explained by constant inclusion with different factors, but not by the same constant-inclusion field and one persistent factor. This example establishes the quantifier distinction; it is not a full FAS model-feasibility test.

## How robustness maps to a recovery allowance

Let the formal original box be `B=product[L_v,U_v]`, with WT interval[1,1], and M the nonnegative codon-unary score plus arbitrary common nondecreasing response. Candidate fields are finite, and actual recovery factors are strictly positive. M is closed under positive rescaling.

1. **WT-relative bound.** If b_w=1 and each b_v lies in[1/kappa,kappa], then existence of a model field f with `b*f in B` is exactly equivalent to feasibility in `[L_v/kappa,kappa*U_v]` with **WT kept at1**. This represents one persistent genotype factor for one completion of the empirical box.
2. **Pairwise spread bound.** If the symmetric factor lambda expands **all** coordinates, including WT, then feasibility is equivalent to one persistent factor with `max(b)/min(b)<=lambda^2`, after normalizing the corrected field to WT=1. The square is a consequence of normalization and free positive scaling. Thus the infimal pairwise-spread allowance is the square of the infimal all-coordinate expansion factor, under these precise hypotheses. It is not the same as the WT-relative cap kappa=lambda.
3. **Metric bounds.** For corresponding infima, `lambda_all <= kappa_WT <= lambda_all^2`. A toy constant-field model with point data(1,K) attains lambda_all=sqrt(K) but kappa_WT=K. It illustrates the convention difference; it is not a reported FAS number.
4. **No joint-scenario substitution.** The quantifiers are “there exist one completion, one model field and one persistent factor.” A rectangular completion may combine extrema never jointly observed. This does not prove that the same factor explains all 27 scenarios or that independently chosen factors for each preparation are persistent. An additional preparation/noise model would be needed for that claim.

Lambda denotes a multiplicative factor, not a fractional percentage radius. If the numerical branch reports delta where lambda=1+delta, the pairwise spread is(1+delta)^2, with excess2*delta+delta^2. An all-coordinate additive radius also permits reference movement, so it is not automatically an anchored relative-score error after WT renormalization. State these conventions beside the computed radius.

A verified radius can therefore provide a **conditional threshold on how much model-rescuing inclusion-extrinsic distortion is allowed**. To say actual true inclusion still violates the null requires both an independently justified nuisance cap and a justified true-field measurement set. Neither is provided by current technical input replication or an empirical range. No biological recovery percentage should be inferred from the selected witness's narrowest separation.

**Zeros and unknowns.** Positive finite multiplication preserves an exact zero. A multiplicative widening cannot turn a zero interval into a positive field; an additive widening can. A sampled count zero need not be a true zero. Unknown[0,infinity] remains uninformative under finite multiplicative widening. Additive epsilon cannot imply a uniform fold bound without a strictly positive score lower bound; the exact synthetic example has absolute change 1/100 at score 1/1000000 and fold factor 10001. A model-rescuing additive radius is therefore not interchangeable with a percent recovery error.

## One orthogonal record requirement

The most informative existing-data record would link the exact eight current certificate sequences to **paired included and skipped RNA quantities from the same independently documented clone/preparation**, with matched WT, a stated RNA population and controls/bounds for differential isoform recovery. Direct inclusion among the relevant isoforms can then separate total RNA amount from inclusion fraction; total RNA and matched intracellular-template measurements would further separate abundance from effective delivery, rather than be silently assumed. If other isoforms exist, included/(included+skipped) alone needs a stated two-outcome interpretation or additional isoform accounting. This would still not identify a kinetic splicing rate.
