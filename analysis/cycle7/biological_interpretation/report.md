## What gamma means under the observation model

Let B be the original coordinatewise empirical interval box, with WT interval [1,1]. Under reviewed Claim 2, gamma is the infimal WT-relative allowance kappa for which there exist **one** model field f, **one** box completion y, and **one** persistent combined genotype multiplier b such that y=b*f, b_WT=1, and each b lies in [1/kappa,kappa]. All three existential objects refer to the same chosen field. At the finite attained boundary, such a formal completion exists. Below it, none exists under those assumptions.

The approximate upper allowance is 2.971596% above unity; the reciprocal lower allowance is about 2.886% below unity. This is multiplicative symmetry, not symmetric additive percent error. The exact permitted factors, rather than rounded percentages, should lead the presentation. The all-coordinate expansion/pairwise-spread convention from Claim 3 is different and must not replace this anchored definition.

The box can combine vertex values from different preparations. The result does not construct one persistent correction explaining all 27 observed fields, even if preparation-specific common response functions are allowed. It also does not estimate b. To interpret a smaller independently justified bound as excluding a true-inclusion null requires a defensible true-field measurement set and a bound on the **entire** inclusion-extrinsic multiplier, not merely RT efficiency. Neither is currently available.

## Strongest surviving alternative

Input and WT normalization cancel source-template representation and shared scales at the ideal intensity level. They leave relative effective intracellular template, total relevant RNA, isoform stability and genotype-dependent input/output recovery. A constant true inclusion fraction can generate any one positive normalized field through unrestricted persistent genotype recovery; legal probability-bounded primitive recovery can be obtained by common rescaling. This is an explicit nonidentification construction, not evidence that such bias occurred or had the computed magnitude.

Consequently, an ordering contradiction could reflect true inclusion interactions, inclusion-extrinsic RNA biology, technical genotype recovery, or their combination. The formal gamma threshold does not choose among them. A common scalar monotone assay response has already been allowed by the null, so that explanation alone is excluded within the stated envelope; genotype-specific mappings or multiple latent processes survive. Sampling uncertainty remains uncalibrated. Nine transfection outputs and three technical input PCRs do not identify their joint covariance or supply a biological error cap.

## Single most informative next record and pre-access contract

The priority is an already existing, sequence-linked record of **included and skipped RNA from the same separately documented clone/preparation for the four non-WT target genotypes plus matched WT**, with differential isoform-recovery calibration or bounds and explicit treatment of other isoforms. It would test whether the new three-order contradiction survives in an inclusion-fraction endpoint rather than included-RNA amount. Total RNA/template measurements would further locate an abundance or delivery alternative. Included/(included+skipped) cancels total amount only with valid isoform accounting and comparable/calibrated recovery; it does not by itself identify a kinetic splice-choice rate.

Before any such outcomes are opened, freeze the target sequences and post hoc selection history; preparation identity and source dependence; RNA population and isoform denominator; endpoint and normalization; complete-target retention, zero/missing handling; marginal or joint error assumptions sufficient for the chosen certificate; order/error criterion; and a result-to-decision rule.
