# Post-primary COMT interpretation and fixed-prediction diagnostics

This protocol is written after the root inspected the primary failure and sensitivity score summaries. It is exploratory interpretation, not a prospective endpoint or replacement validation. The full numerical comparison is temporarily stopped at a reference encoding mismatch. No result here enters the manuscript before that issue is independently resolved.

Retain both preparation maps, heavy and fixed mid sensitivities, all125 targets,53 sites,113 SNVs, both regions, all five models and every prediction/error. Do not refit, change folds, alter normalization, tune parameters, remove sites or select a favorable assay. Input only the sealed actual_v3 contrasts, predictions and evaluation.

Questions: (1) Which resolved RNA/protein-proxy sign disagreements survive both maps? (2) Does any one assessment site drive the primary model comparison? (3) Are the fitted models ill-conditioned or extrapolating? (4) What can be said about assay substitution without a biologically calibrated tolerance?

For every target, save both maps' R,T,L,L_mid centers and full empirical ranges. Report the entire sign classification, overlap of resolved opposite-sign cases across maps, and all unresolved cases. For a resolved R/T disagreement, let m be the smaller distance of those two contrast ranges from zero. Expanding each underlying assay log range by delta changes a contrast endpoint by2delta. The disagreement is guaranteed for delta<m/2 and becomes non-resolved at equality. This is an unstructured sensitivity radius, not a noise estimate or confidence limit; shared-reference constraints can narrow the Cartesian set. Report all targets, including zero radii for unresolved/no-disagreement cases, and do not pick a radius to declare biological importance.

Export all saved per-site errors. For each site, delete only that site's assessment errors from aggregation and recompute the equal-site MAE on the remaining fixed predictions. These are leave-one-assessment-site-out summaries without retraining, not leave-one-site-out cross-validation or independent replications. Retain comparison against every baseline and both maps. Count how many such summaries meet the original operational10% rule, explicitly without treating any as rescue.

Check each frozen linear fit's condition number independently by singular values of its centered weighted predictor matrix, using the saved training identities and site weights. RNA-only has one nonconstant predictor; a second constant-zero load column is not a genuine added feature. Compare exact declared ranks and record all extrapolation flags and coefficients. Do not exclude unstable folds. This verification is a distinct diagnostic, not another fit.

The closest substantive prior is the originating integrated COMT MAVE, which already reports cross-layer heterogeneity. The new question is the declared site-heldout predictive comparator and map sensitivity. Shared-measurement algebra R=r-d,T=d-f,L=h-r was fixed before outcomes; a load predictor can cancel RNA measurement noise. No favorable prediction or opposite sign by itself identifies compensation or translation flux. Low-bin enrichment is not mean protein amount. Without an authenticated practical substitution tolerance, classify the missing equivalence claim as unresolved; do not invent an equivalence margin from observed errors.

All outputs preserve exact stored-input error fractions. Log-derived range radii are ordinary floating calculations and must not be described as exact molecular quantities. Any mismatch in identities, existing scores or condition diagnostics stops and is preserved.
