## Fixed F0 eligibility audit

The seven-source shortlist above is fixed before any new phenotype
evaluation. Record reference/construct, individual nucleotide identity,
translation, single-nucleotide edge semantics, intended versus observed
coverage, replication, raw counts and aggregation. Source metadata may
establish intended coverage but cannot establish phenotype usability.
Known FAS and Cambray outcomes remain historical and are not new
validation. Cambray is recorded as a retired comparator outside the
seven-source new eligibility denominator.

Do not fit phenotypes, enumerate outcome-selected complete subsets or
invent intermediates during this gate. A single-codon library can offer
within-codon paths without identifying cross-codon interactions. A
regulatory motif library, even with a fixed reference protein, is not a
coding-synonymous mutation graph. Stop the initial atlas at a seven-source
decision table. Expand only if an authenticated library design makes
additional eligible combinations plausible.

## Exposure rules

Primary article text and previously published examples are known.
Metadata acquisition can download source containers, but only explicitly
allowed identifiers, sequence columns, header schemas and preparation
records are decoded before a pilot freeze. Any incidental outcome access
is recorded. Outcome absence in metadata is not usable-data coverage.
No new experiments, interventions, sequence optimization, clinical claims,
author contacts, external publication or history rewriting is authorized
by this plan.
