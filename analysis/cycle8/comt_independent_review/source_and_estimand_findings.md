# COMT A1 source and estimand preflight

Status: source/estimand component reviewed before source count bodies or phenotype values. Final protocol/runtime/model candidates are pending immutable hashes; this file does not authorize outcome access or certify implementation.

1. **Source identity supports the narrow object.** The A0 contract authenticates the full expressed fusion and exact nucleotide identifiers, including translation of the retained synonymous calls. The 125 shared IDs at 53 codon sites and fixed 113-SNV sensitivity are source-ascertained metadata cohorts, not a new random biological sample. Holdout by complete codon site appropriately protects alternative codons at a seen site. It validates the fitted calibration within these source regions, not new stable cell lines, experiments, constructs or native COMT isoforms.

2. **Target sign is descriptive.** Negative low-moxGFP/mCherry-bin enrichment points toward less representation in the source's low-fluorescence bin. It is not a measured positive protein concentration, mean fluorescence or translation flux. RNA/polysome induction and FACS induction differ, and mCherry-relative fluorescence can change for reasons other than COMT molecule number. Consistent assay orientation is necessary but cannot establish assay substitution in biological units.

3. **Reference coupling is substantive.** For R=r-d, T=d-f, L=h-r, independent centered raw assay noise gives Cov(R,T)=-Var(d), Cov(R,L)=-Var(r). RNA and target are not unrelated independently measured endpoints, and load can remove RNA measurement error. The exact saved synthetic example has constant true heavy/RNA load but joint RNA+load prediction error zero versus nondecreasing PAVA MAE 1/2. Even a large held-out gain therefore supports only prediction of the named proxy using these derived measurements, not translational compensation or a load-mediated mechanism.

4. **Unpaired envelopes are permissible descriptive objects.** Biological group summaries and interval differences can be computed without asserting cross-assay pairing. Cartesian bounds preserve a conservative unpaired interpretation but are not confidence intervals or simultaneous biological uncertainty sets. Reusing d or r in several contrasts does not make their separately drawn boxes jointly realizable. Both recorded FACS label interpretations must remain reported. Their agreement does not establish that either is the true stable-line crosswalk or cover every possible mapping.

5. **New runtime reconstruction is a different computational provenance claim.** Historical classifier/normalization equivalence remains unauthenticated. A newly frozen RF reconstruction can define a prospective operational proxy, conditional on retaining the original failed historical gate and all differences. Filtering all relevant background alleles before W=DP-sum(CAO) is essential; using only the 125 targets changes the denominator. Raw source REF/ALT representation must be preserved for source variant-type rules before canonical prediction-ID joins. Nonpositive corrected frequencies remain unavailable; filtering them does not demonstrate biological zero or lack of expression.

6. **Capability and comparison scope.** A joint constrained RNA-linear+load-linear fit and its nested RNA-linear baseline repair the obsolete sequential PAVA-residual saturation problem. Weighted nondecreasing PAVA supplies a stronger named univariate comparator but does not represent every RNA-only function or the MAE-optimal calibration. The training-only ROI median baseline is a cheap necessary safeguard against a pooled-model gain that merely captures region-specific processing/normalization. Save within-ROI errors as well. Do not extrapolate the named algorithm comparison into a conditional-information theorem.

7. **Coverage and evaluation.** Retain all 125 targets and all 53 sites in coverage reporting, including source-stage filtering, missing biological groups and nonpositive subtraction. All compared models must use the same eligible training/assessment cases; publish both RNA/target-only eligibility and joint-feature eligibility so a favorable complete-case subset is visible. Technical repeats are not additional biological groups. Equal weight per codon site must govern both fitting and final assessment aggregation, rather than equal weighting of folds with different site counts. Any 10% gain/100-variant/40-site rule is an operational resource criterion, not a practical equivalence margin or calibrated statistical rejection.
