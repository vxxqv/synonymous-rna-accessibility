## Reporting and operational limitations

Two useful reporting summaries remain derivable from the preserved records but are not standalone fields: RNA/T-only eligible variant/site counts versus the common load cohort, and per-fold assessment errors. Report the former explicitly when presenting coverage so a load-induced dropout is not disguised as RNA failure. Do not silently redefine the fitted cohort to provide it. The full per-variant errors, site errors, fold identities and assay-completeness flags already permit both summaries without refitting.

`driver.py:121` has a bounded RF timeout, but `TimeoutExpired` occurs before the normal execution-log write. A run controller should preserve the exception's captured output, failed/partial directory and stop status. No successful stage seal is written on this path. Do not rerun another seed, extend the timeout after seeing biological predictive performance, or treat a timeout as a biological null.

Preparation labels and mappings do not establish independence or paired assays. These are ascertainment-dependent synonymous variants in one reporter/source study. The target is negative low-bin representation in nats, not physical protein concentration. Shared measurement error can produce perfect load-model gain with constant true heavy/RNA load, as the prior exact synthetic counterexample establishes. A positive prediction result would remain an algorithm-specific descriptive result, not compensation, causal incremental information, clinical evidence or assay substitution. No universal performance or novelty claim is warranted.
