# G0 kinetic decision

**Decision: no-go for kinetic fitting, identified kinetic compensation, or a structure-mediated mechanism from the current COMT proxy exports.** A frozen proxy-level assay-transfer question can remain eligible under its own source and uncertainty gates. Agreement or discordance among its scores is not a kinetic parameter estimate.

The exact positive steady-state fiber is proved in derivation.md: even perfectly calibrated nuclear RNA, cytosolic RNA and protein amounts leave three free parameters. Common time rescaling explains only one. This report is an internal algebra/source gate, not a new empirical result or independently reviewed theorem.

The source contract identifies induction at 24 h for RNA/polysome and 21 h for cytometry, not a calibrated simultaneous equilibrium triplet. The tagged reporter and its fluorescence need not equal an endogenous protein amount. Source normalization includes shared counts/controls and across-variant preprocessing. Preparation crosswalk ambiguity is separately retained. Solving that ambiguity or using conservative marginal uncertainty would not repair kinetic nonidentifiability. Conversely, lack of paired covariance is not itself a reason to discard a properly qualified conservative proxy-level comparison.

## Two proxy falsifiers

At a synthetic fluorescence cutoff of 1, equal probabilities 1/2 at (1/2,3/2) or at (1/2,7/2) give the same low-bin probability 1/2 but different means 1 and 2. More low-bin mass need not even imply a smaller mean: masses (3/5,2/5) at (1/2,10) have low-bin probability 3/5 and mean 43/10, while masses (2/5,3/5) at (1/2,3/2) have probability 2/5 and mean 11/10. A single bin therefore does not identify an abundance mean or its direction. These examples concern exact hypothetical probabilities and already refute the implication before score normalization, enrichment or sampling uncertainty is added.

Likewise, even ideal steady-flow occupancy m=J times residence time would identify only their product if that relationship and complete, non-aborting flow were assumed. Synthetic pairs (J,residence)=(1,2) and (2,1) give the same occupancy; (1/2,6) gives more occupancy with less completion flux. COMT metafraction scores do not authenticate the additional assumptions or supply those physical factors. This is a mathematical counterexample to the proxy implication, not a rate model fitted to the study.

## What additional constraint types would change the decision

First an authenticated observation map must connect the measurements to the relevant compartments and protein product in a compatible state. Then independently informative throughput/partition and protein-clock constraints are needed. For example exact nuclear removal, cytosolic degradation and protein degradation, alongside exact n,c,p, reconstruct all six parameters with an explicit positivity check. Known synthesis/export/protein-production fluxes provide another sufficient information set. These are identifiability requirements only; this gate proposes no new experiment or intervention. Uncertainty sets for such constraints would give a parameter set, not automatic point identification.

Do not infer kinetic mechanism from a proxy predictor, a synonymous full-protein identity, a folding score or an untranslated RNA pool hypothesis. Those may motivate explanations but do not select a member of the mathematical fiber. No structure feature was computed or assessed here.
