## Replication, aggregation and endpoint limitations

**Schmitz.** Twenty bins are different observations from one sorted pooled population, sharing cell totals, sequencing denominators, normalization and distribution fitting. The three Sort-seq experiments are the relevant repeated source operations, not 60 independent bins. Independent library construction, transformation and culture-day structure are not fully authenticated from their labels. Ten individually isolated/Sanger-checked clones are validation material in the source, but exact matching to any future complete neighborhood and preparation independence remain unresolved. Public figure captions and published coverage/filter counts are already exposed, not a new holdout.

The measured endpoint is fluorescence of matured reporter populations under the specified growth/harvest/sorting process. A fitted noise statistic is not a direct translation rate. The primary Results call the plasmid SC101/approximately five copies, whereas Library construction describes a p15A origin; record this construct-description discrepancy rather than silently select one. A full plasmid sequence/map would close the origin/reference contract. It does not invalidate the explicit variable-codon design by itself.

**TP53.** The Methods name two replicate scores but do not by themselves settle whether these are independently integrated libraries, independent cultures, or another preparation level. Shared WT normalization and per-bin cell-count reconstruction create dependencies. The 7,500 threshold is source-defined and correlation-selected, not an uncertainty-calibrated guarantee. A bin-weighted GFP score measures the fusion reporter and its processing; it is not TP53 fitness or native full-length protein activity. The paper's clonal RT-PCR/Sanger analyses identify selected splice products, but cannot automatically authenticate every pooled variant's actual isoforms. Reference-synonymous graph membership therefore does not prove preservation of every protein actually produced.

No raw-count likelihood, calibrated interval, replicate covariance or complete retained-graph denominator can be declared without the actual schemas. Numerical row counts in article prose are not a substitute for a count-only coverage inventory under a future approved contract.

## Scope filter

Retain synonymous coding objects and nucleotide edge semantics. Reject interpreting bin count, sequence count, cell count, PCR index or replicate label as interchangeable biological units. No clinical, causal, evolutionary-accessibility or native-protein-function conclusion enters. This is a fixed two-source completion of F0, not an exhaustive estimate of how common complete synonymous landscapes are.

## Object filter

Schmitz's 4,096-state design is a valid symbolic complete product, while measured coverage remains gated. TP53's doped design is not prematurely reduced to singles, nor promoted to a measured combinatorial graph. Both require actual nucleotide records and processed-product caveats. Neither missing access nor outcome filtering is a biological negative. No favorable graph, endpoint, precision rule or subgroup was chosen.

## Conclusions

Both sources retain plausible nucleotide-level information, unlike amino-acid-only DMS, but neither currently meets the complete **measured** neighborhood contract (S1-S4,T1-T4). Schmitz has the stronger authenticated intended graph. TP53 has an explicitly named full data/notebook deposit and a strong minigene-processing caveat, but its files are presently inaccessible through the attempted normal public route. Their gate status is unresolved, not ineligible biology.
