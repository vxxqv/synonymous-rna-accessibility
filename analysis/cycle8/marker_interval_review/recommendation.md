# B1 recommendation: finite marker-context descriptive pilot

**Qualified GO to freeze a descriptive all-30-label protocol; HOLD isolated-edit, factorial mechanism, full-haplotype and calibrated population claims.** This is a mathematical/source-contract recommendation, not authorization to open outcomes. The source already demonstrates marker non-neutrality, so another selected example is not an adequate new question.

Retain all 30 metadata-eligible, reference-annotated synonymous intended labels with multiple canonical marker states: 24 have two states and six have three. Evaluate the complete declared states separately in KBM7 and K562. These are 60 label-cell targets, not 60 independent biological units. Their 66 marker states per cell and three source-labelled rows per state are metadata expectations, not verified finite-outcome coverage. Canonicalize words/aliases before analysis, preserve the R1207R sequence discrepancy as an identity hold, and never turn an absent context into a zero. Untested markers remain outside any finite observed-set conclusion.

Freeze the response and normalization first. A positive local cDNA/gDNA proxy ratio and its log are different numerical range scales. Pin pseudocounts, denominator scope, cell/amplicon matching, invalid/nonpositive response rules, and a practical tolerance independently of observed values. A log-range tolerance can be described as a fold-spread tolerance only on an authenticated physical ratio scale. A processed standardized score needs its own units and interpretation.

Use a complete per-state descriptive envelope with a declared interpretation. Missing declared contexts receive the full uninformative domain. Compute both sharp range bounds using distinct upper/lower markers, and retain certified invariant, certified noninvariant and unresolved labels under the weak rule R<=delta. Report all 30 denominators, cell-specific coverage and all marker identities. Do not drop predicted bad markers, maximize across favorable subsets, infer population prevalence, or call empirical replicate envelopes confidence intervals. Conservative simultaneous marginal coverage, if independently authenticated later, does not require a guessed paired covariance; current metadata supplies no such calibration.

The additional two-cell question can be a prespecified shared-marker-order diagnostic, with exact weak-tie and interval feasibility semantics in derivation.md. Forced order reversal is interpretable as failure of a common marker ordering across the two bundled cell contexts. An acyclic possible-order graph means only compatibility with one interval completion. It is not validation of a common scale, an isolated edit, or identical marker effects. Any prediction exercise must split by whole intended label before values and retain shared marker/genotype/preparation dependence; it is optional and cannot replace the descriptive denominator.

WT and marker-only bounds may identify combined-versus-marker contrasts and relative interaction differences if comparable endpoints exist. They do not identify an intended-only effect under unrestricted edit-marker interaction. A bounded-interaction conclusion must name the response scale, absolute bound, calibration source, Cartesian assumption and positivity restrictions. Do not infer that bound from the observed combined-state range. The present gate does not authenticate a comparable WT response or fix the labelled T-marker motif.

Stop the stronger interpretation if the local identity/replicate join is unresolved, the interval construction lacks the claimed coverage, full marker-state outcomes are missing, or the weak decision remains unresolved. Still retain every finite combined-state observation in the qualified descriptive report under its frozen missingness rules. No source claim is made about complete realised haplotypes, all processed products, total RNA, protein function or clinical effect.
