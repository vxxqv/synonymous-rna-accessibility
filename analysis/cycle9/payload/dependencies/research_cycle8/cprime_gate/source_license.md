# cPRIME source data attribution

Ghanem El Kassem, Constanze Westphal, Marvin Doebel, Jasmine Hillmer, Tanja Seifert and Michael Boettcher (2026), *cPRIME enables endogenous mapping of genetic variant effects at RNA level*. Nature Communications. DOI: https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-026-78344-9 .

The originating article's permissions statement identifies the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International license: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/ . Supplementary Data 8 is retained unchanged as a computational input. The XLSX contains18 XML/package members and no embedded image, macro or publishing PDF. Selected sequence/assay metadata files are derived column selections, not edited source measurements. Acquisition URLs, original container and member hashes are preserved in the adjacent receipts. Primary article prose and figures are not redistributed here.

The workbook is an already public source dataset, copied as opaque bytes before the B1 outcome freeze. Its presence is not evidence that outcome values were inspected before the authorized analysis. The production reader is restricted to the declared396 row instances and paired RNA/DNA/score fields.
