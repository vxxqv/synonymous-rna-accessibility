This is a small, source-conditioned ordinal finding in exposed data. Its sensitivity margin is narrow and its source normalization prevents a physical RNA/protein trade-off interpretation.

The complete 134-pair comparison ran once after protocol, implementation and synthetic results were sealed. No source normalization, assay value, variant eligibility rule or frozen prediction criterion was changed. ## The retained pair and its exact scope

With a=`c.330C>G` and b=`c.330C>T`, the differences are:

| Contrast | Minimum over both maps and all joint choices | Maximum |

| DeltaR, RNA relative to gDNA | 0.01485348125525654 | 0.23126675899978366 |
| DeltaT, gDNA minus low-bin representation | -1.8569720193801107 | -0.05449090490206476 |

Units are natural-log source-normalized proxy differences. Thus a ranks above b in DeltaR and below b in DeltaT in every one of128 declared joint cases. The pair has edit-string distance one and both alleles are SNVs relative to the authored reference. This is not an evolutionary path, fitness measurement or native-isoform claim.

For independent perturbations up to delta of each allele's assay-group log representation, the strict reversal survives only for

`delta < 8361766580791/2251799813685248 = 0.003713370313814135...`.

At equality a tie is permitted. This is the exact worst-case radius of the stated rectangular perturbation model on stored numeric values. It is not an empirical noise estimate, confidence interval, calibrated biological tolerance or bound on source bias. No cutoff was chosen to classify this small radius as important.

The normalization challenge materially qualifies the result. The assay-level RNA differences a minus b are negative in all four groups, approximately -0.376 to -0.227; the DNA differences are more negative, approximately -0.458 to -0.390. Subtracting the DNA contrast creates the positive RNA-relative-to-DNA ranking. These are already source-normalized assay representations, not raw molecule counts. The FACS difference also varies substantially between source preparation groups and maps, although its contrast against DNA remains negative after the prescribed orientation. Consequently the result establishes an ordinal mismatch of the operational readouts, not a normalization-independent physical trade-off.

The synthetic case zR=zF=0, zD=-1 passes the same strict-reversal predicate. It proves that such a predicate alone cannot identify intrinsic compensation: a shared DNA contrast can generate opposing signs. Further, variant-specific negative-control subtraction generally prevents complete cancellation of a common position-WT denominator. These limitations were fixed before the observed comparison, not introduced to select or rescue its result.

## Closest-prior disposition

Hoskins et al. already reported directional/divergent cross-layer expression signatures, site-specific effects and RNA-versus-FACS comparisons. A source-specific claim of previously unknown generic discordance is rejected. The final article's inspected body and pinned analysis narrative do not explicitly report this site149 alternative-versus-alternative universal-preparation comparison; the manuscript/author-code search found no equivalent named contrast. This is a bounded within-source comparison, not a global novelty claim, and the originating data already contain the two variants.

Cycle8's four stable variant-versus-WT targets were at sites50,52,63. This site149 pair answers a different local ordering question and is retained because every selected within-site pair was tested, not because it was picked from favorable outcomes. The mathematical inference is elementary: strictly reversed ranks cannot be represented by a common nondecreasing mapping in that represented field. No new generic order theory is claimed.

Recommended use is a concise example of conditional local readout non-substitution, accompanied by the full table and its narrow margin. It should not replace the central negative prediction result or become a claim of robust biological compensation. The original complete prediction rule remains failed under both maps; some individual sensitivity comparisons still pass exactly as previously reported.
