The bounded literature verdict is **partial or adjacent result only** for the proposed full local pair inventory, with strong overlap for its biological motivation. This is based on the originating final article, its methods and pinned author analysis, not an exhaustive global novelty search. The order-reversal implication itself is a formal elementary consequence of monotonicity. No priority claim for a new mathematical method is proposed.

The source used one engineered COMT fusion and four stated stable-line groups per ROI, with additional technical/preparation repeats. The preserved code/GEO FACS grouping disagreement concerns20 of47 labels. Both maps reuse measurements and cannot count as replication. RNA/polysome induction and cytometry timing differ. Complete metadata translation authenticates synonymy for the authored fusion, not all native isoforms or realized haplotypes. WT reconstruction and control subtraction are operational source rules rather than validated molecule counts.

The strongest possible supported sentence, conditional on the unopened comparison result, is: “Among the selected same-codon synonymous alternatives, specified pairs retain opposite RNA-representation and oriented low-bin-proxy rankings across every represented preparation combination under both source-supported grouping maps.” It must immediately retain the shared-normalization and same-source limitations. “Synonymous variants impose a biological expression trade-off” would be too strong without new measurements.

The strongest falsifiers are an equivalent already reported local comparison, a reversed or nonuniform map/preparation case, missing matched technical support, source-normalization dependence, or an unverified clone/product identity. A positive proxy result would not falsify RNA processing, reporter maturation, recovery, protein turnover or denominator coupling. A null would not show that all local biological effects are absent.
