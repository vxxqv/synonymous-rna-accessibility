# Closest precedents and the additional question

| Established result | Relationship to this pilot | Permitted additional claim |
|---|---|---|
| [Julien et al. 2016](https://doi.org/10.1038/ncomms11558) measured mutational effects and interactions in FAS exon splicing. | Functional effects and epistasis in this exon are prior discoveries. | A new analysis must quantify a precisely defined same-protein route constraint; it cannot claim first discovery of FAS epistasis. |
| [Baeza-Centurion et al. 2019](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cell.2018.12.010) constructed the 3072-state evolutionary combination library and investigated global scaling and specific effects. | This is the source dataset, not independent validation. | Restricting to the native included-protein class provides an exact 72-state synonymous domain, whose empirical order and accessibility can be audited against an unrestricted monotone additive null. |
| [Scott 1964](https://doi.org/10.1016/0022-2496(64)90002-1) applied finite linear-inequality solvability to measurement representation. | The mathematical ancestry of additive order representation and cancellation certificates is established. | The interval implementation and its application to this measured domain are a reproducible analytical construction, not a new foundational representation theorem. Publisher abstract verified; exhaustive full-text priority review is not claimed. |
| [Otwinowski and colleagues 2018](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6094095/) inferred monotone global-epistasis models from sequence measurements. | A nonlinear measured response alone does not establish specific interactions. | Exact exclusion of every nondecreasing response consistent with an empirical envelope is narrower and more diagnostic than poor fit of one chosen sigmoid. It remains conditional on the measurement envelope. |
| [Brookes, Otwinowski and Sinai 2024](https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper_files/paper/2024/hash/a9b938e79504889f905d549f8d53e405-Abstract-Conference.html) modeled global epistasis using ranking losses. | Ranking rather than raw-scale regression is an established approach. | The pilot returns feasible or cancelling rational witnesses instead of a predictive ranking benchmark. No superiority or algorithmic-priority claim is made. |

The biological question is whether a fixed native-protein subset retains an assay-level ordering or accessibility constraint after codon geometry and arbitrary monotone scaling are allowed. A positive result would concern the deposited included-RNA assay. It would not alone distinguish splice regulation from genotype-dependent RNA abundance, recovery or amplification. A negative result would limit the proposed interpretation, not establish biological neutrality.

The current pilot deliberately makes no prospective prediction of unseen variants. Repeated transfections share one constructed library. The 2016 material is a possible later same-exon reproducibility check, with design dependence and incomplete overlap to be audited before its outcomes. It is not a substitute for an independent exon or independently sampled biological system.
