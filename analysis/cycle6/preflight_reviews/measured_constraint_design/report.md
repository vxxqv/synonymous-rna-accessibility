# Decision: measure a separator, certify a bounded constraint

**Yes, sparse measurements can support a useful exclusion without a complete phenotype landscape or paired covariance. The priority should be a source/measurement contract, not a new mathematical headline.** The strongest useful extension is a calibrated statement that every acceptable synonymous route must leave a specified sequence neighborhood, with a lower bound on the extra point mutations. It does not assert that an unmeasured route exists or is absent. The ingredients are established certificates, graph distance and simultaneous error control.

## Exact contract and argument

Let G be the authenticated single-nucleotide synonymous graph, including every allowed allele and edge under the declared code, peptide, background and move rule. Let distinct endpoints s,t lie in the same baseline component, with finite graph distance d. Specify a finite domain D containing them, and one biological endpoint in a fixed condition. For example, mu(v) can denote population-mean relative growth in the Cambray rich-condition assay. Acceptance mu(v)>=tau must have an explicit biological/operational interpretation; it is not automatically fitness, clinical benefit or a selected evolutionary trajectory. A PSI or expression measurement cannot silently replace that endpoint.

For a fixed measured index family M, suppose valid marginal bounds satisfy

`Pr(mu(v)<L(v)) <= alpha_v^-` and `Pr(mu(v)>U(v)) <= alpha_v^+`, with the sum of all used error budgets at most alpha.

There is then a simultaneous coverage event with probability at least1-alpha, regardless of dependence between genotypes, assays or a shared reference. The budget sum must cover the entire eligible selection family, not just the ultimately reported cut. Use only a lower bound where acceptance is needed and only an upper bound where failure is needed. The bounds themselves must account for the correct sampling units, systematic error/normalization and any selection or stopping. Fixed-n confidence limits do not automatically survive optional stopping. A cut may be selected after inspecting bounds if simultaneous coverage was established over the whole prespecified selection family. Unadjusted marginal95% intervals are insufficient.

On simultaneous coverage, define known failures F={v:U(v)<tau}. Give every other vertex, including every unmeasured one, the benefit of being acceptable. If s and t are accepted by their lower bounds and G[D]-F has no s-t path, no truly acceptable route remains wholly in D. This is a deterministic zero-certificate: any proposed route would contain a certified failing vertex. A known acceptable path analogously gives a positive certificate. If the optimistic graph still connects, report unresolved. Weak acceptance requires strict U<tau to exclude a vertex; U=tau is not failure. For several traits or acceptable intervals, form conservative interval boxes and retain any vertex whose intervals intersect the acceptable set; the same outer-graph reasoning applies, without estimating cross-trait covariance.

The precise additional consequence is useful when D is the *complete* graph-distance ball B_R(s), R>=d. Every acceptable global route must visit a vertex w at distance at leastR+1 from s. By the triangle inequality, d_G(w,t)>=R+1-d. Its length is consequently at least

`2(R+1)-d`,

or at least `2(R+1-d)` additional moves beyond a shortest baseline route. This applies to routes with temporary mutations, backtracking or repeated vertices; it does not restrict the search to shortest mutation orders. It is an elementary graph-distance consequence of the certificate, not a new graph theorem or a rate/dynamics prediction. If D is only a sampled subgraph or a two-allele face, only departure from that domain is certified, not the distance-ball bound. If G itself disconnects s,t, report structural disconnection; no phenotype-induced finite barrier has been demonstrated.

## Minimal informative pattern and damaging alternatives

In the complete synonymous graph of three Lys codons, encode AAA/AAG as0/1. This is Q3. Observe only s=000,t=110 and the intermediates100,010. Suppose the endpoint lower bounds exceed tau and both intermediate upper bounds are below tau. Those two failures separate the endpoints inside B_2(000), even though three other vertices in that ball are unmeasured. Therefore any globally acceptable route has at least four point mutations and must temporarily change the third codon. Global connection is unknown: of the16 assignments of acceptable/unacceptable status to the four unmeasured vertices,13 disconnect globally and3 permit a four-step route. Thus the identical observations support a necessary detour, not global impossibility. This example is synthetic, not an instruction for physical sequence work.

The binary-domain premise matters. On K3 x K2, failed intermediates10,01 do not rule out00->20->21->11. An unobserved third allele bypasses the apparent square restriction in three steps without a third position. The full coding domain, not a chosen pair of alleles, determines the certificate. Measuring only one failed intermediate or letting either upper bound equal tau also destroys the two-step exclusion.

For a **single scalar trait on an actual Q2 spanning two different codon factors**, the strict four-corner ordering also rejects an exact codon-additive field and any common nondecreasing transform of one. An additive latent field obeys a00+a11=a10+a01. A monotone map that puts both endpoints strictly above both intermediates requires min(a00,a11)>max(a10,a01), contradicting that identity. On the measured scale the interaction contrast has lower bound L00+L11-U10-U01. This is established reciprocal sign epistasis in the assayed trait, not an RNA-folding mechanism. Two variable nucleotides within a single codon do not suffice to reject arbitrary codon-level unary effects. It is stronger than the previous *joint* obstruction: the exactly additive pair (x-y,-x+y) already gives individually connected traits with a disconnected intersection. The prior94-95% smoothness attenuation and additive antagonism therefore remain direct threats to an RNA-specific interpretation. A validated scalar ordering can reject a comparator class; it cannot identify why it fails.

## Covariance: sufficient marginal control versus unidentified reliability

Paired covariance is **not required** for the conservative certificate above. Pairing and genotype/context identity remain necessary where the endpoint definition requires them; covariance is a separate statistical issue. Shared-reference uncertainty can be included as another bounded input and propagated by interval arithmetic. Ignoring it is not justified by subtracting or dividing by the observed reference. Conservative bands may be wide, but width is an information limitation, not a reason to demand an unavailable joint distribution as a prerequisite to every valid inference.

In contrast, marginal probabilities do not point-identify route reliability. On a diamond with two internal vertices, each acceptable with marginal probability1/2, connection probability can be1/2,3/4 or1 under positive dependence, independence or anticorrelation. Statistical confidence in a conservative certificate is not a posterior probability that the biological network connects. Four95% marginal coverage statements can likewise have only80% simultaneous coverage under disjoint failure events. The union-bound adjustment is essential.

Exact binomial limits are available *if* an endpoint is genuinely independently sampled Bernoulli with fixed sample size. The one-sided Clopper-Pearson bounds at zero or n successes are U=1-alpha_i^(1/n) and L=alpha_i^(1/n). Cross-genotype independence is unnecessary for Bonferroni combination. PCR reads, cells and biological preparations are different units; integer counts or UMIs alone do not validate these formulas. A conditional finite-preparation RNA-pool proportion is also distinct from a population mean across cultures. Clustering, selection and bias require a justified model. None is inferred here.

## One retrospective design criterion and stop rules

Freeze a biologically meaningful tau, practical margin delta>0, endpoints, complete bounded domain and candidate separator K before target evaluation. Check whether the public source actually measures the endpoints and *every* member of K with a defensible marginal-bound model. A successful constraint requires L(s),L(t)>=tau+delta and U(v)<=tau-delta for all v in K, with total error allocation at most alpha. It need not measure all other vertices. This is a criterion for an existing measurement design, not a proposed new experiment.

| Result of that gate | Permitted decision |
|---|---|
| Identity, complete graph/domain and marginal error model authenticated; separator covered and strict margins pass | Report the bounded exclusion or necessary detour, with its stated error control and condition. |
| Valid bounds overlap threshold | Inconclusive resolution; do not call the biological hypothesis false. |
| An essential cut member is unmeasured, or an omitted allele/edge opens the optimistic domain | Design uninformative for that exclusion; retain unknown paths. |
| Coding identity, endpoint meaning, sample units or a valid marginal model unavailable | Not identifiable under the source contract; stop before a new model/outcome search. |
| Dependence unknown but the marginal bounds and selection family are valid | Proceed conservatively; do not impose an unnecessary covariance gate. |
