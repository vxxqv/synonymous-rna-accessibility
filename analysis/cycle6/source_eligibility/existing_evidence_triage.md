## Scope and object filters

The question is a defensible measured synonymous effect or quantitative limit. New models, raw-read reconstruction, additional folding, changed thresholds, genotype subsets selected on effects, and repeats of completed prediction questions are excluded. Native-gene perturbation measurements cannot substitute for synonymous construct contrasts. A numerical estimate is not automatically an independent replicate; the independent unit must be a documented physical preparation or culture. A different endpoint in the same source is not a new study.

The relevant objects are exact construct-to-measurement joins and replicate/control definitions. Graph completeness is required only for the particular path statement being made. It is not necessary for observing the effect of a single sequence change. Conversely, abandoning graph completeness does not recover uncertainty, a missing construct identity, or causal mechanism. None of the inspected schemas justifies a general confidence bound excluding biologically meaningful synonymous effects. Any empirical replicate range would be a descriptive range, not such a bound.

## Ranked route1: original single-mutant culture reproducibility

**Question.** Within one prespecified reporter/promoter/medium, do the deposited original culture-specific estimates for every sequence-verified synonymous single mutant give a reproducible relative-fitness departure from WT? Report all measured cases, signs and between-culture spreads, including disagreements. A useful outcome can be a quantitative limit on what these *reported culture estimates* establish, even if no calibrated effect inference is possible.

**Why it is viable.** S4 explicitly exposes `Genotype`, `PTDH3_replicate1..3`, `PTDH3_pool`, and corresponding PAGP1 columns. The original Methods state that independent competitive cultures were used separately to estimate single-mutant fitness. Thus the missing double-mutant covariance is irrelevant to this narrowly chosen question. The prior v3 eligibility already allowed descriptive fitness effects; this is a refinement of that permission boundary, not a discovered new biological effect.

**Unit/control/normalization contract.** A biological culture, within a fixed reporter/promoter/medium and timepoint, is the repeat. Technical day0 sequencing repeats, barcodes and genotype rows are not extra cultures. WT is the common normalization denominator and equals1 by construction. Source fitness is `w=[(ft/f0)/(Ft/F0)]^(1/g)`. Count pooling is not an arithmetic mean of culture fitness. Do not use the pooled column as an extra replicate or infer its error from scatter around an arithmetic mean. A shared WT creates dependence between mutants. Replicate spreads are descriptive unless the per-culture count/generation/WT model is authenticated.

**Identity and selection contract.** Authenticate the S4 genotype against the pinned first ORF in `work/primary_biology/Ref_GFP.fasta` or `Ref_URA3.fasta`, then retain all synonymous singles under a predeclared assay and source-selection scope. The original source requires day0 counts>=100 and describes discarding fitness<0.5; exact pre/post-pooling order and the WT SE/SD filter conflict are unresolved. A result must be conditional on deposited source-selected entries. No missing value is zero and no subcutoff value may be silently removed to repair provenance. Do not switch among conditions after seeing their values.

**What would be new.** A transparent measurement-consistency audit and explicit information bound for this project. The source already studied synonymous fitness effects, so a same-source confirmation is reproduction, not novelty or independent-study validation. Three cultures can establish a descriptive repeated measurement; they do not establish a folding mechanism or robust population-wide effect prevalence.

**Falsifier/stop.** Unresolvable genotype/control alignment; replicate labels that cannot be attached to the declared culture design; unresolved numerical contradictions affecting the chosen estimand; or a requested calibrated confidence claim without its noise/selection model. Failure to reproduce a consistent sign remains a result, not grounds to select another condition.

**Minimal next permitted action.** Metadata only: write an immutable S4 assay/column/control map, audit exact genotype identities and the prior exposure boundary without decoding fitness cells, and verify whether source documents distinguish replicate-wise and pooled filtering. Root can then decide whether to freeze a retrospective descriptive protocol. A calibrated effect claim requires additional provenance beyond the present headers. No outcome run is authorized by this report.

## Ranked route2: separate PERSIST measured protein endpoint

**Question.** For the source's complete prespecified protein-assay panel, can synonymous constructs with identical actual translated products and fixed UTR/modification/formulation be compared against the same reference using measured NanoLuc/Firefly activity across identified independent experiments? This asks about measured output, not another prediction of the RNA coefficient.

**Exact contract.** Primary Methods describe co-transfection of10ng NanoLuc RNA with20ng HBB-Firefly control, assay of both luminescence signals, and their ratio. They state a minimum of three independent experiments and removal of signal peptides for the actual protein-assay templates. Pfizer assays further normalize to the NanoLuc start reference. Figure4 and Figure5 are not exchangeable: source text assigns technical triplicates to the formulated-RNA panel. Do not treat its wells as three independent biological experiments, or pool protein assays with different modification/formulation/times. Common Firefly/reference normalization must remain explicit.

**Evidence pointer and missing object.** `work/persist_cycle3/methods_only.xml`, sections `Cell culture and transfections` and `Luciferase activity assay after mRNA transfection`; article supplementary map to `41467_2022_28776_MOESM4_ESM.zip`. The pinned author construct table is a useful starting identity source, not proof of the protein-assayed sequences after signal-peptide removal. The required object is a panel-by-actual-construct-by-experiment/well map with control and normalization metadata. That object has not been authenticated in the inspected project records.

**What would be new.** A measured protein endpoint separate from the completed cellular-coefficient/MUP test. The original investigators selected and tested candidates and published those panels, so this cannot be advertised as prospective candidate success, a fresh study, or unbiased validation of their selection. It could supply a tightly scoped biological corroboration or exclusion only after the identity contract passes.

**Falsifier/stop.** Assayed CDSs encode different proteins, or CDS changes are inseparable from UTR/modification/formulation; no common reference or condition; no experiment-to-well map; only derived predicted expression rather than measured luminescence; only selected successful constructs retained. Any of these closes the proposed synonymous contrast.

**Minimal next permitted action.** A new root-authorized metadata-only inspection of the already identified supplementary panel index and construct/preparation annotations, with no numeric luminescence or missingness inspection. This audit did not acquire the supplement. If a complete controlled pair/panel cannot be authenticated, stop rather than substitute the37 cellular-decay constructs.

## Conclusion and uncertainty

Only route1 currently has locally authenticated replicate-labelled numerical fields and a direct synonymous functional interpretation. Its remaining work is a measurement contract and its likely contribution is careful reproduction, not a new mechanism. Route2 is a credible distinct measured endpoint but remains an identity/preparation gate. No existing result should be relabelled as a successful predictor. No quantitative absence-of-effect or pathway allocation claim is licensed by the present source metadata. The ranking favors a small source audit over new computation; it could change if the PERSIST assay map fails or original S4 selection semantics cannot support even the declared conditional question.
