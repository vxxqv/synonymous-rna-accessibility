# Human-cell synonymous screens: bounded source triage

Status: secondary methodological references, not the selected accessibility pilot. No raw counts, phenotype tables, guide sequences, or notebook outputs were opened. Published aggregate findings and examples returned by search are exposed. This is adaptive source selection, not a prespecified validation study.

## Rao and colleagues, 2026

The [primary paper](https://doi.org/10.1093/nar/gkag733) studies growth in human cell lines using base-editor guide libraries. Its estimates operate at guide level, with editing efficiencies predicted and control distributions used in a mixture model. Counts are compared with the initial library. Public resources are PRJNA1236376, [synSg](https://github.com/ryy1221/synSg), and Zenodo records [15241793](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.15241793) and [21109071](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.21109071). The [September correction](https://doi.org/10.1093/nar/gkag855) concerns presentation and figure resolution.

Decision: do not convert guide measurements into an exactly measured genotype graph. Multiple edited alleles and incomplete editing require an explicit measurement model. A future reanalysis of effect prevalence would need assay sensitivity, target dependence, validation-selection rules, and uncertainty in fitted reference distributions. Those requirements have not been verified here. The published rarity result is prior evidence, not a new result of this project.

Provenance: public PMC XML version 1 has SHA256 `049485eb9525ca135efc1c9aab8871cca7a8ddab01916391fd6eb48ceb186a69`; the inspected repository tree is `e7d0ea7e59012956c3f4f2769ea4dc6415c25da9`. Tree metadata alone does not establish executable or statistically calibrated analysis. The XML receipt is versioned and may precede the corrected publisher presentation.

## Niu and colleagues, 2025 online / 2026 issue

The [primary paper](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41587-025-02710-z) uses prime-editing screens to study synonymous effects on cellular fitness and investigates selected mechanisms. The authors already developed a predictor. Raw sequencing is identified by HRA007615; processed data and code are linked at [Zenodo 14639522](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.14639522). The public archive inventory lists a 228.7 MB ZIP with MD5 `7be9438c6c3d151e3751f41ed4dd7481`; its preview reports that it is too large. It was not downloaded.

Decision: retain as mechanistic and assay-design prior. No complete synonymous combination landscape or genotype-level replicate contract has been authenticated. Do not acquire a large archive merely because it is recent. Revisit only if exact source metadata identifies combinations and independently interpretable measurements needed by a frozen question.

## Research decision

Prioritize the FAS native-exon combination design for a separate sequence and measurement-contract audit. It can potentially connect synonymous geometry with a measured RNA-processing phenotype. Human-cell growth screens remain relevant biological context, but cannot currently validate a multistep accessibility claim. No source restriction was bypassed: publisher access failures were left unchanged; PMC's documented public Open Data metadata and XML service supplied the open-access methods receipt.
