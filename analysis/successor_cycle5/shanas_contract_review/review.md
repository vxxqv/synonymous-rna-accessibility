## Strict synonymous full-CDS question

The available evidence supports a **current authentication no-go**, not proof that no synonymous pair exists. The primary Methods place the reporter cassette before the GFP stop codon. The publicly recovered 210nt library fragment and 1316nt splice-mapping reference are not an authenticated promoter-specific full coding sequence. A legacy sequence literal and unused upstream constant do not establish the intended 2026 coding start, phase, terminal stop or protein consequence of included, skipped and alternative splice isoforms. Therefore synonymous edge and square counts remain **not computed**, not zero.

All 9608 annotated cassette exon lengths are 2 modulo3. This makes a blanket claim of protein preservation particularly unsafe; it does not itself prove that every within-isoform sequence pair is nonsynonymous, authenticate the reading phase, or establish actual isoform abundance. The source does not justify calling the cassette untranslated. Recovery of exact context-specific coding constructs and declared splice-isoform translation would be the necessary next evidence if the strict full-CDS question were retained.

Primary Methods describe native designs as 2x134, while the recovered `cassette_filtered` inventory is 264/132. That discrepancy requires an explicit inventory/definition qualification; it cannot be assigned to phenotype dropout, because no outcome missingness was inspected.

## Conditional matched-cassette context transfer

A separate RNA-processing question is coherent without protein preservation: can a prespecified predictor or admissibility rule for an identical, barcode-matched cassette transfer to a prespecified promoter or genomic-context preparation, for related-sequence groups not used in fitting? The entity must include library ID, barcode, cassette sequence, construct context, sample/preparation provenance and identical endpoint definitions. Identical147nt variable fragments alone are insufficient to establish identical transcribed reporters. Some promoter constructs may share transcript sequence, while this cannot be generalized to every promoter/context from the cassette identity alone.

This is at most **conditional metadata feasibility**. Shared finite measured coverage, replicate-level uncertainty and evaluable heldout units have not been inspected. No validation denominator or eligible graph count can yet be reported. A matrix containing the same design IDs in every intended context does not establish measurements in every context.

| Threat | Required boundary before a new outcome analysis |
|---|---|
| Duplicate/related cassettes and barcodes | Freeze sequence/parent-aware groups, including unlabelled and combinatorial designs. Gene labels alone cannot certify independent sequence families. Keep same cassette and all its contexts on the appropriate side of a family split. |
| Repeating original promoter findings | Specify a genuinely heldout context/family prediction target and fixed comparators. Original promoter dependence, motif effects, context comparisons and stratified prediction already exist. |
| Apparent external replication | Call a shared reporter-library, cell-line and preprocessing holdout within-study context transfer. Paired sequencing files are not distinct biological samples. Sample labels alone do not prove culture independence or pairing. |
| Preprocessing and batch dependence | Pin sample-to-construct mapping, biological versus technical preparation hierarchy, endpoint formula and source filtering. Training-only normalization is required if claimed; deposited global preprocessing cannot retrospectively become training-only. |
| Missing observations and graph scope | Authorize a separate coverage-only gate under frozen rules before model outcomes. Incomplete measurement is unknown, not negative, and sampled components do not exclude unmeasured routes. |
| Calibration or mechanism | Repeated preparations may support descriptive reproducibility. Biological uncertainty, practical admissibility and a promoter-specific mechanism require their own justified evidence; they are not supplied by source reuse. |

At the inspected author notebook calls, `StratifiedKFold(5, shuffle=True, random_state=42)` is supplied to `cross_val_predict` without gene/context groups. A stricter family/context split could be a distinct generalization test, but absence of groups at these calls is not proof that every published analysis lacks grouping or that such a new split must outperform the source. It does not confer novelty on promoter dependence itself.

The strongest alternative explanation is a portable underlying cassette response observed through context-specific, sequence-dependent measurement or preparation bias. This can mimic a context-transfer failure without a new regulatory mechanism. A common strictly increasing transform of an already computed scalar field preserves endpoint-relative ordering, but unequal preprocessing before averaging, genotype-specific errors and filtering need not. The evaluation target and calibration claim must therefore remain separate.
