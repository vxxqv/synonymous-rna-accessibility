## Source processing and identifiable dependence

The following deductions come from authenticated author code read as text, never executed. Source hashes and exact line pointers are in `source_manifest.json`.

**Protein, `protein_processing.R`:** lines 13-20 correct each bin using a shared total, its bin count total and sorted-cell frequency. Lines 124-139 select four regular-initiation preparations and use a maximal three-bin window with minimum-depth argument zero. This differs from the four-bin Methods description. Lines 148-178 fit replicate scaling, remove low observations classified as probable mutants and quantile-normalize columns. Lines 186-214 apply fitted exponential transformations, further rescaling, clipping and a 1-to-100 representation before reporting the row mean and variance. Thus the deposited variance is across cleaned processed columns, not a supplied calibrated standard error.

There is an additional precise replay gap: line 199 constructs its first two output columns using the same input column 2, with an affine rescaling for the second, rather than using columns 1 and 2 separately at that step. Earlier reordering also complicates nominal preparation-to-column identity. **This proves a property of the deposited script, not duplication in the deposited phenotype table.** The actual generating revision has not been authenticated. Do not repair it silently or assume four statistically independent processed estimates. Root was notified before any outcome access.

**Growth, `growth_processing.R`:** lines 24-40 replace low counts by missing and normalize by sample totals. Lines 46-49 define the relative-growth transformation from final and initial frequencies and generation count. Lines 111 and 175-177 reuse the initial R0 reference across R60 lines. Lines 131-173 estimate shared generation/calibration quantities using additional time comparisons. Lines 224-231 fit across-variant rescaling jointly with the follow-up time points, and lines 282-290 write the three R60 columns and their mean. Thus choosing the R60 columns does not isolate them from source follow-up calibration. The script also has unresolved aliases/intermediate objects and an unrelated reference at line 191, so its text alone does not establish a complete generating replay.

Consequences are shared denominator noise, composition-induced between-variant dependence, common calibration uncertainty, and nonrandom filtering near low counts or low protein observations. Growth branch variation is biological propagation variation conditional on its founder, not merely sequencing noise; founder/library uncertainty is not independently replicated. Filtering can make complete cases systematically different from all sequence-selected designs. Missing is not nonfunctional, and zero counts must not be replaced by zero growth or a failed vertex.

**Normalization does not automatically create the graph phenomenon.** A common strictly increasing transformation or offset applied to a complete trait field preserves endpoint-relative connectivity. Such a transformation within each preparation also preserves that preparation's ordering test. Clipping/quantile ties, filtering, genotype-dependent corrections and averaging preparations after unequal or nonlinear transformations need not have that invariance. In particular, transforming before averaging is different from transforming the resulting mean. Dependence alone is not proof that a classification is an artifact; these specific non-invariant operations remain competing explanations.

## What uncertainty is and is not available

| Target | Current eligibility | Limitation |
|---|---|---|
| Marginal GFP dispersion | Can later describe the finite processed preparation values | Small nominal n, global fitted processing, clipping/filtering and column-version gap prevent automatic confidence calibration |
| Marginal R60 dispersion | Can later describe line variation conditional on source founder/reference | Three lines do not estimate between-founder variation or uncertainty in their common baseline/calibration |
| Between-variant measurement covariance | Whole preparation vectors retain some descriptive information after authorization | Complete-case sample covariance has rank at most 3 for four GFP columns and at most 2 for three growth lines; one library and few preparations do not identify a general covariance model |
| Cross-phenotype biological error covariance | Not identified | Unpaired measurements do not reveal how culture-level GFP and growth errors co-vary; across-genotype correlation mixes true sequence effects and noise |
| Joint biological confidence for graph claims | Not calibrated by the inspected source contract | Twelve cross-products, empirical extrema and technical count intervals supply no calibrated simultaneous coverage over traits and vertices |
| Between-study or natural-population uncertainty | Unsupported | Within-library heldout seeds do not reproduce library construction, environment or biology independently |

Absence of paired data is not a universal mathematical prohibition on conservative confidence statements: valid marginal simultaneous bounds could be combined by a union bound without knowing cross-assay covariance. Here those marginal bounds and their processing/selection calibration are themselves not established. Likewise, matching marginal distributions would not identify a same-culture joint success probability without a dependence assumption. Do not mistake either problem for one solved by an assumed independent Gaussian error model.

Observed preparation minima/maxima and whole-column cross-products remain useful **sensitivity sets**. An optimistic/pessimistic connectivity certificate is deterministic conditional on that set containing the relevant true quantities. It is not an empirical confidence region, and success over those scenarios cannot establish a confidence level. The existing protocol already makes this distinction and should retain it.

## Practical thresholds and strongest permissible conclusion

No source-authenticated external practical minimum was recovered for a GFP difference, a joint shortfall or a relative-growth difference in this intended reporter application. Arbitrary units rescaled across a library cannot by themselves supply a molecule count or biologically required output. Growth in a pooled competition is not an absolute doubling time or a universal natural fitness threshold. The paper's measurement resolution and observed variance do not define practical importance.

The frozen endpoint minimum is a defensible **operational question**: preserve at least the lower observed endpoint in each trait on a declared graph. It is not an externally justified acceptability requirement. The 10-square/eight-group feasibility minimum and 10% prediction hurdle are analysis rules, not power calculations or biological effect margins. They must not be reduced after outcomes. Quantitative resolution requires a separate pre-outcome synthetic assessment over explicitly declared dependence/error assumptions; this source-only report supplies no numerical power result.

Without additional calibration, a future positive could support category B from the user brief: a descriptive local restriction with native-unit margins, dependence-preserving sensitivity and exact graph scope. Category A requires a justified reproducibility/uncertainty contract beyond scenario agreement. Category C, a practically negligible effect, is not currently identifiable without an externally justified margin and adequate resolution. Unknown coverage or uncertain classification gives D or E, not a biological null. A measured acceptable detour can overturn an observed-component claim; no missing path certifies the unobserved biological space.

**Single most informative missing measurement:** an authenticated replicate-level paired GFP/growth measurement vector from independently prepared material, preserving genotype IDs and the shared-reference/unnormalized observation records. That would directly address joint error dependence and founder/preparation variation. More reads, more threshold cells or more overlapping squares cannot replace it. Recovering the exact original generating revision and sample manifest is the cheapest existing-data improvement, but would not manufacture pairing that was never measured. No new experiment or sequence intervention was designed here.
