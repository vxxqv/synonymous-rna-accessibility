## Role of mathematics and alternative explanations

Exactly additive antagonistic traits can produce a joint-only obstruction. Therefore such an observation does not itself establish epistasis, an RNA-folding mechanism or an evolutionary constraint. The existing missing-corner additive and simple-local benchmarks remain mandatory, including failures. Four observations support one interaction contrast; fitting the four observed values does not establish predictive generalization. Arbitrary composition or sequence-context models are not added to compensate for limited coverage.

The rank bound applies only to the specified product graph, additive model, trait scales and required codon intermediates. On the measured binary square, the exact Hadamard residual gives an a posteriori error over those four observed values. It is not a uniform bound on latent biological function or on unobserved synonymous genotypes. Exact square reachability already has a two-intermediate solution, so this bound is not new predictive evidence there. No noisy numerical rank is interpreted as a biological dimension. The larger deposited components and unobserved sequence space do not inherit a product or uniform-error assumption automatically.

The primary endpoint is a property of genotype-aligned measured trait profiles, not authenticated culture-paired measurements. Independent phenotype shuffling would destroy their across-genotype association. A fixed-endpoint square control that merely exchanges the two intermediate profiles leaves existence of a route unchanged and is not an informative null test. Generic additive antagonism, shared preprocessing, incomplete routes and source uncertainty remain explicit alternatives. No additional correlation model or permuted p-value is introduced without an identifiable design and a separate pre-outcome specification.
