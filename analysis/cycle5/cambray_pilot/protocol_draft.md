# C5 Cambray joint function pilot

Status: draft awaiting independent design review, sequence-only split checks and a freeze manifest. No new phenotype analysis is permitted until those gates close.

## Question and contribution boundary

Do complete, intended-synonymous four-genotype reporter designs contain pairs of endpoints for which reporter output alone and relative growth alone each admit a path, while requiring both on the same path removes both mutation orders? Does any such restriction survive preparation sensitivity and observed detours? Can a local additive approximation predict the unobserved corner better than simple alternatives on reserved design families?

The biological quantities are processed GFP reporter output and pooled relative growth in the matched normal-initiation, rich-MOPS context. More GFP is a declared reporter-output requirement, not universally higher biological fitness. This is a retrospective, prospectively specified reanalysis of an existing experiment. It is not a new independent experiment, natural evolutionary landscape, structural mechanism test or intervention implementation.

Closest priors already establish expression-growth costs (Cambray2018; Frumkin2017), paths across several measured protein traits (TtgR2021), and joint functional constraints under inferred protein-family scores (Kirsch2026). The candidate additional observation is a fully enumerated measured synonymous path conflict with explicit sensitivity and missing-state boundaries. Generic bottleneck algorithms and additive antagonism are known explanations.

## Primary point endpoint

For each of the two opposite-corner endpoint pairs s,t in every eligible square, define the requirement for trait k as tau_k=min(f_k(s),f_k(t)). A vertex is acceptable for that trait when f_k(v)>=tau_k, including exact ties. This asks whether a path maintains at least the lower endpoint's observed output for each trait. It does not assert a natural selection threshold or clinically meaningful acceptability range. The property is invariant to strictly increasing transformations applied to each trait, so it is not selected by arbitrary assay-unit scaling.

On the complete square, classify: jointly connected; disconnected for at least one individual trait; or joint-only obstruction, meaning each individual trait connects the same endpoints but their intersection does not. The primary descriptive endpoint is the number and fraction of all eligible diagonal pairs with joint-only obstruction, with square, design-seed and vertex dependence explicitly retained. Report numerator and denominator by arm and seed. Both diagonals, including every negative result, are mandatory. No binomial independence model is used.

No threshold is chosen after inspecting outcomes. As complete sensitivity, report the exact two-dimensional threshold regions for all tau_1<=min endpoint trait1 and tau_2<=min endpoint trait2, split at observed critical values. Represent each critical equality as a singleton and each interval between distinct values as an open interval. The cell below the minimum observed trait value is unbounded and constant in acceptability; do not integrate its infinite area or invent a probability weight. Only critical-value cells that can change the classification are needed. Summaries across cells are geometrical descriptions, not probabilities under a natural threshold distribution. Also report native-unit tradeoff pairs: the shortfall below the endpoint minimum in each trait for each alternative intermediate. Do not collapse them into one biologically calibrated score using arbitrary scales.

## Additive and prediction benchmarks

A complete square has four observations per trait and an additive model with three parameters, leaving one interaction contrast. Report the exact contrast y00-y10-y01+y11 and its Hadamard additive projection in the original processed scale and, only when all four trait values are strictly positive, natural-log scale as a declared secondary representation. Log ineligibility never removes a primary square. This is descriptive decomposition, not independent predictive evidence. Interactions and joint obstruction are distinct: the known exactly additive opposing-trait positive control produces a joint obstruction.

For the prediction feasibility test, lexically sort the four corner IDs, join them with `|`, prefix `C5-corner-v1|`, encode as UTF-8 and interpret the full SHA256 digest as a hexadecimal integer. Its remainder modulo4 indexes the sorted list to choose the withheld corner. Predict that corner from the other three using (a) the additive completion, equal to the sum of its two adjacent corners minus the opposite corner; (b) the arithmetic mean of its two adjacent corners; (c) the arithmetic mean of all three known corners. No parameters are tuned on the reserved families. Do not clip a negative additive prediction to a favorable range. Report all errors, MAE and RMSE separately per trait and arm; aggregate squared/absolute errors within each seed first, then average over seeds equally. A square is one prediction case, not four independent held-out observations.

To prevent shared-corner leakage, form the union of all selected withheld IDs within each arm before any outcome read. A square is prediction-eligible only if it contains exactly one ID in that global mask. Others remain in the primary descriptive analysis but receive a prediction exclusion reason. Do not change the hashed corner choice to recover coverage. With joined seeds, prediction errors are aggregated equally over related seed groups rather than individual seeds. The entire prediction computation receives only the three allowed corners as input; it cannot query another square or a full phenotype table.

Process each arm in two sealed stages. First parse the seven required replicate columns only for allowed known-corner IDs and generate all three methods' predictions for every structural exactly-one-mask case with finite three-corner inputs; do not parse withheld-corner phenotype fields, target finiteness, complete-square eligibility, author means, graph-tolerance maxima or component outcome summaries. Hash the numerical predictions, source, metadata mask and manifest. Only then unseal the required arm outcomes, determine complete-case coverage, retain missing-target prediction failures, and compute primary descriptions, error diagnostics and prediction scores. Validation begins only after the development report, unchanged source and predictions procedure are recorded. This is conditional missing-corner prediction, not sequence-only prediction of a wholly unmeasured scaffold.

Predict the joint-only classification on the diagonal for which the withheld corner is an intermediate, using the same two observed endpoint values. Report the complete confusion table, balanced accuracy where both classes occur, coverage and every failure. A one-class evaluation is explicitly uninformative about class discrimination and cannot pass the discrimination screen. The prediction screen separately requires at least10 eligible prediction squares and8 related seed groups in validation. The additive approach passes only if reserved-family group-balanced MAE improves by at least10% over each simple baseline for both traits and its group-balanced binary misclassification rate is no worse than each simple baseline. If a baseline MAE is zero, strict superiority is unattainable; report absolute differences and a null relative-gain value rather than dividing by zero or declaring a tie successful. Ten percent is a prespecified modeling hurdle, not a calibrated biological impact threshold. Failure is preserved; no new predictors are fitted to this validation set.

## Decision and stopping rules

The pilot supports a limited measured path-conflict result only when coverage passes and at least one preparation-consistent diagonal appears in each arm. Report its effect margins and observed-component status; do not automatically call that a broad or practically important biological mechanism. A stronger range certificate is reported separately and is required before the phrase 'robust to the observed preparation ranges'. No observation establishes RNA folding, structural occupancy, evolutionary selection or engineered function improvement.

If only point classifications survive, report descriptive threshold geometry with unresolved measurement uncertainty. If none survive, distinguish absence on this measured domain from information limitation or instability. A failed predictive screen does not erase a descriptive path observation, and vice versa. Do not extend to additional traits or favorable environments using this validation set. A successor requires a new source-grounded question or new independent material, and a new exposure record.

The primary agent alone integrates, commits or publishes. Final deliverables include the complete unit and exposure tables, code and exact checks, every preparation/control result, a claim-to-evidence decision, and a figure only after the scientific conclusion is stable.
