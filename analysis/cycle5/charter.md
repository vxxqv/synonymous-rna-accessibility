# Research charter and adaptive cycle

Goal: determine a measured biological constraint on synonymous sequence change, or a quantitatively supported exclusion, and connect it to a verified mathematical statement. Journal quality is an aspiration, not an operational endpoint or a promised publication.

Scope: synonymous sequence change under biological function constraints. Object: joint functional acceptability, splicing routes and context dependence. Subjects: source-authenticated finite variant graphs and compatible replicated measurements in the user-nominated studies.

Knowledge: deep knowledge of the existing project's conditional mathematics and completed failures; working knowledge of its source gates; source-specific knowledge for the newly nominated studies is initially incomplete. The first phase is exploratory source and closest-prior review. Later descriptive protocol design and retrospective execution are separate recorded stages.

Questions:
1. Which datasets recover measured synonymous edges, complete local landscapes or explicitly bounded routes with compatible functions and meaningful replication?
2. What observation could distinguish joint functional restriction from a standard scalar bottleneck, assay noise, missing variants or context differences?
3. Which mathematical restrictions permit a new sharp characterization or a useful inference bound, and which are already subsumed by known reconfiguration or vector-optimization results?
4. Can the result survive the strongest baseline, dependence-aware uncertainty, a meaningful validation boundary and independent numerical/proof review?
5. What additional measurement would most reduce the unresolved inference if the currently available data cannot answer the question?

## Adaptive research loop

Each candidate progresses through proposed, source-screened, eligible, protocol-frozen, executed, independently-verified, adversarially-challenged and decided stages. A decision is supported, hypothesis-failed, model-failed, information-limited, access-limited or superseded-by-prior. Stages cannot be promoted solely because a result is favorable.

If no branch reaches a practically meaningful, supported result, perform another targeted literature and evidence cycle. A successor needs a materially different biological question, independent material or newly resolved measurement limitation; larger models, new thresholds, favorable subsets and repeated tuning on examined validation outcomes do not qualify. Retain failed and indeterminate branches in the same public evidence register.

Practical effect criteria must be justified in the assay's units or externally defined acceptable range before new outcomes are examined. Threshold sweeps are complete sensitivity surfaces, not a search for a favored threshold. Replicate uncertainty and gene/family transportability are separate. A mathematically sharp impossibility or identification result can be useful without positive phenotype prediction, but its novelty and empirical relevance require explicit comparison.

At most two biological execution branches and one mathematical development branch run deeply at once. Literature/metadata screening can span all nominated routes. Candidate ranking considers importance, accessible independent units, discriminatory power, closest-prior gap and expected information per computation. Expensive analyses require a passing feasibility and identifiability gate. Stop individual branches when their information target is reached or no justified next test remains. Do not promise indefinite computation or eventual favorable results.
