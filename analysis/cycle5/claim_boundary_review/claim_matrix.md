## Decisive mathematical counterexamples

**Joint conflict is compatible with exact additivity.** On Q2 let F=(x1-x2,-x1+x2), s=00 and t=11. Both endpoints are(0,0), and the intermediate profiles are(-1,1),(1,-1). Each trait has its own two-edge route, but no intermediate passes both traits at threshold0. For each trait the interaction contrast y00-y01-y10+y11 is exactly0. Therefore observing the proposed graph pattern cannot establish epistasis. Its effect rank is1 and eta=0; it also requires no RNA-folding mechanism. Correlation, antagonism or the existence of an ordering conflict alone is not a new mathematical principle. A transparently measured instance can still be a descriptive biological observation at its declared scope.

**The pattern is ordinal, while additivity and rank are not.** A common strictly increasing map g_k applied to each final vertex-trait summary preserves f_k(v)>=min(f_k(s),f_k(t)); hence it preserves the individual and joint graphs. It can change the interaction contrast and projected rank. For two initially identical traits z=x1+2x2, replace the second by z cubed. The primary graph classifications are unchanged, while the second trait's interaction contrast becomes18 and the projected effect matrix determinant becomes-3 instead of0. Thus nonlinear scale choice can create a nonzero interaction without changing the primary observation. Transforming series before averaging is a different operation and need not preserve the mean field. Weakly monotone maps can merge ties. These caveats do not license calling shared normalization the confirmed cause of a barrier.

**Local and full graph have opposite evidential directions.** A positive measured path is an existence witness in every supergraph retaining its scores. A missing path in a sampled graph is not a missing path in that supergraph. The reviewed Q3 additive example has a locally obstructed Q2 but a zero-compromise route through a temporary background coordinate. A complete measured square therefore authenticates its two mutation orders, not every synonymous route.

**No calibrated resolution follows from an arbitrary epsilon.** The symmetric square is universally joint-only obstructed over the full error box iff m>2epsilon, with the hypotheses specified in the amendment. This says nothing about the actual assay error radius. At equality ties can open a route. Without authenticated epsilon, a practical margin and a coverage argument, neither absence nor small observed margins imply negligible biological effects. The fixed-low-end range certificate and12-series consistency are separate criteria with no automatic nesting; neither calibrates the other.

## A and C are gated, not reachable by stronger wording

Category **A** needs additional authenticated evidence establishing reproducibility at the claimed experimental unit, context, preprocessing and graph scope. Independently prepared matched material or an independent study could supply part of that evidence, but more genotypes, all-group-deletion stability or twelve dependent combinations cannot. Independent culture/study validation and within-library family holdout must always be named separately. The current source contract does not establish A.

Category **C** needs a prespecified practical effect target, validated uncertainty with appropriate simultaneous coverage, and adequate resolution/graph coverage to exclude effects above that target on the declared domain. Calibrated simultaneous marginal bounds can sometimes be combined without cross-assay pairing; pairing is not a universal mathematical necessity. Here neither those calibrated marginal bounds nor the practical margin exists. Counts, empirical minima/maxima, an assumed covariance, a post-target Hadamard residual and missingness simulations cannot substitute. The current source contract does not establish C.

The ordinal requirement is preservation of two deposited assay summaries relative to their endpoints. More reporter output is not universally improved fitness, and no construct, route or experiment is being optimized. Terms such as “functional improvement,” “engineered improvement,” “evolutionary accessibility” or “mechanistic constraint” need evidence outside this contract. “Measured restriction of the two local mutation orders under the declared processed-trait requirements” is the strongest safe generic formulation after the relevant gates succeed.
